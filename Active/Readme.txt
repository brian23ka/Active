Vagus & Nyabuti Limited
==============================

Company website for banana distribution in Kenya.

Pages
-----
index.html             Home
about.html             About the company
services.html          Banana distribution
portfolio.html         Banana supply
team.html              Supply approach
blog.html               Banana guide
contact.html            Supply enquiry form

The contact form is handled by forms/contact.php. Configure the CONTACT_EMAIL
environment variable with the company's receiving email address and ensure PHP
mail delivery is enabled on the hosting server before publishing the form.
